# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the
AppDimens community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards
Positive behavior includes using welcoming and inclusive language, being
respectful of differing viewpoints, gracefully accepting constructive
criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable
behavior includes sexualized language, trolling, insulting/derogatory
comments, and personal or political attacks.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing behavior may be reported to the project team
via GitHub issues (marked `conduct`) or private security contact in
SECURITY.md. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly.
